✨NEW✨ uwuMaps 1.0.0 for Android
maps.uwuapps.org

New:
- The whole of uwuMaps as a native Android app, not a web page in a window
- Material You: your phone's colours and font, or switch to the usual uwuapps look in Settings
- Offline maps downloaded straight to your phone, carrying on if they stop
- Spoken turn-by-turn directions with no connection
- Send a place or route to a phone nearby with a QR code, no connection needed
- uwuMaps links open in the app, and it updates itself

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/uwuMaps/releases/latest
