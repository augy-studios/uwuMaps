Walking directions in Singapore can now take you through void decks and under covered walkways, instead of keeping to the road, with or without a saved map.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwumaps-1.0.2.apk` |

On an iPhone, a computer or any other phone, use [maps.uwuapps.org](https://maps.uwuapps.org) in the browser, and add it to your home screen to install it as an app.

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install without scanning**.

**Already have the app?** It updates itself. Tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

## What's new

### Walking
- **Through void decks**: walking routes in Singapore can now cut through the void decks of HDB blocks, and the directions say which block. These crossings are worked out from where the blocks and paths are rather than surveyed, so very occasionally one may be walled off. If it is, walk around and the route updates.
- **Under cover**: covered walkways and linkways are worth a small detour, so routes prefer them. Shopping mall corridors now count as places you can walk.
- **No download needed**: this works in Singapore without saving the map first. Saved maps get it once they're updated in **Offline maps**.

### On the website
- **The same as the app**: the website now has everything the app has: the map following you, the compass, your speed, the tilt handle, two-finger panning, **Check for updates** and **Scan a code**.
