The first uwuMaps app for Android: colourful 3D maps and directions that work with no connection, in your phone's own colours and font, with offline maps straight to your phone and an Edge Tray always at hand.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwumaps-1.0.0.apk` |

On an iPhone, a computer or any other phone, use [maps.uwuapps.org](https://maps.uwuapps.org) in the browser, and add it to your home screen to install it as an app.

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install without scanning**.

**Updating later**: the app updates itself. Tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray, for a page of its own with what's new in each version.

The app keeps its own saved places and settings, apart from your browser's. To bring your saved places across, use **Saved places** > **Sync between devices** with the same code on both.

## What's new

### Everything on the website
- **The whole of uwuMaps**: 3D buildings in colour, search, directions on foot, by bike or by car, turn-by-turn navigation, saved places, sharing, and public transport for Singapore and Malaysia, including which train doors stop nearest the lifts and escalators. It all works offline wherever you've saved the area, except short share links, sync and Malaysia's live trains and buses, which need a connection.

### Android app
- **Made for Android**: the app is drawn natively rather than being a web page in a window, so the map, the panels and the buttons all move as Android's own apps do.
- **Material You**: the whole app in your phone's colours and your phone's font, light or dark as your phone is, and drawn the way Android's own apps are. Colours from your wallpaper need Android 12 or later.
  - Turn it off in **Settings** in the Edge Tray for the usual uwuapps look, with its glass, its Jua font, its seven colours, and light, dark or time-based mode.
  - Or keep Material You and turn off **Your phone's colours** or **Your phone's font** on their own.
- **Offline maps, straight to your phone**: pick an area in **Offline maps**, and a stopped download carries on where it was. A saved map stays until you delete it.
- **Spoken directions**: your phone's own voice says what's next, and the screen stays on while you're navigating.
- **My location**: asked for the first time you press **My location** or start from where you are. It stays on your phone unless you choose to share a live ETA.
- **Send to a phone nearby**: show a QR code, which needs no connection at all, and scan one with **Scan a code**, which asks for the camera when you first use it. Or send by code, with a connection on both phones.
- **Links open in the app**: uwuMaps links you're sent open here rather than in your browser.
- **The Edge Tray**: the same column of buttons at the side of the screen, with **Check for updates** added, sliding off the edge when you want the whole map.
