Easier to move around the map: two fingers now pan in any direction, the map follows you with your dot in the middle of the screen, the compass points the map the way you're facing, and your speed shows while you're cycling or driving.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwumaps-1.0.1.apk` |

On an iPhone, a computer or any other phone, use [maps.uwuapps.org](https://maps.uwuapps.org) in the browser, and add it to your home screen to install it as an app.

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install without scanning**.

**Already have the app?** It updates itself. Tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray.

## What's new

### Moving around the map
- **Two fingers pan**: drag with two fingers in any direction to move the map. Pinching still zooms, and twisting still turns it.
- **A handle to tilt and turn**: press the new button beside the zoom buttons and drag. Up and down tilt the 3D view, and left and right turn it.

### Following you
- **Your dot stays in the middle**: press **My location** and the map moves with you, with your dot in the middle of the screen the whole way, and during navigation too. Drag the map to look around, and press **My location** to come back.
- **The compass points the map the way you're facing**: press the compass button and the map turns as you turn. Press it again to face north and lie flat.
- **Your speed**: shows on the map while you're navigating by bike or by car. With no route, it shows once you're going faster than a walk.
